# Museeve — logo exploration, batch 2: type-led

**Date:** 2026-08-11 · **Tool:** LoveArt · **Supersedes:** batch 1 (rejected in full)

## Why batch 1 died

Client verdict: **"flat, corporate, cold"** and **"they just look AI-made."** Not the concepts,
not the palette — the *register*. All ten were flat, two-tone, symmetric, geometrically
constructed marks, which is the default look every image model reaches for when it hears
"logo." Ten ideas, one aesthetic. Self-inflicted: every prompt in batch 1 banned gradient,
shadow, dimension, texture and colour range up front, so warmth was impossible by
construction.

**Chosen register: type-led with attitude.** The name is the logo. Precedent is the entire
history of music branding — Motown, Blue Note, Def Jam, Warp, Factory. Cheapest identity to
build, the hardest to fake, and impossible to make "cold" if the letterforms have a point
of view.

## What makes these not-AI

Specificity of reference and a **formal move**. Not "a modern wordmark" — a named tradition
plus one concrete thing the letters *do*: fuse, compress, splice, echo, stretch. A prompt
with a formal move produces a decision; a prompt without one produces an average.

Each of the ten below has exactly one.

## Before you run these — the spelling problem

MUSEEVE is seven letters with a doubled E in the middle. Image models drop repeated letters
and hallucinate extra ones. Expect **30–50% of generations to be misspelled.**

- Every prompt below already carries a spelling clause. Keep it.
- **Judge the letterforms, ignore the spelling.** You are harvesting a typographic voice,
  not a file. A perfect-looking misspelled roll is a *win* — note the seed.
- Nothing here ships. The winner gets rebuilt from a real licensed typeface, then kerned and
  redrawn by hand. These prompts choose a direction; they don't produce artwork.
- Run 1:1 or 16:9 depending on the mark's proportion. Keep every seed.

**Run 07 and 06 first.** Those two are furthest from anything a model produces by default.

## 01 — Soul warmth · *formal move: the letters fuse*

Detroit/Memphis soul label. Warm, generous, physically close.

```
A wordmark reading MUSEEVE, set in a fat soft-cornered sans with swollen bowls and counters
squeezed down to narrow slits — the warmth of a 1960s Detroit soul record label. Every letter
touches its neighbours with no space between them, so the whole word reads as one continuous
mass. The two adjacent E's fuse into a single wide double-E sharing one long middle crossbar
that runs through both. The baseline tilts very slightly upward, optimistic. Warm cream
#F2E4D8 letters on a deep red #8B1C1C ground.
The word is spelled with exactly seven letters in this order: M, U, S, E, E, V, E — note the
doubled E in the middle. One word, one line, no other text anywhere in the image.
No outline, no drop shadow, no thin modern sans, no evenly-tracked tech wordmark, no icon,
no circle badge, no tagline.
```

## 07 — The echo · *formal move: the second E is a delay of the first*

**The most ownable idea in either batch.** The doubling in the name becomes the doubling in
the mark — the logo says the name out loud. Works as a whole visual language: the echo

```

letter is fully solid. Off-white #E7E7E7 letters with the echoed E in muted crimson #8B1C1C,

The word is spelled with exactly seven letters in this order: M, U, S, E, E, V, E — note the

```

## Judging batch 2

Different criteria from batch 1 — a wordmark is judged on voice, not on silhouette:

1. **Say what it sounds like.** If the client can only say "nice," it failed. If they say
   "loud," "expensive," "underground," "friendly" — that's the direction, and it's the real
   output of this batch.
2. **Ignore spelling errors entirely** at this stage.
3. **Cover the word.** Would the letterforms alone, with the name unreadable, still feel like
   this brand? That's whether it has a voice or just a font.
4. **App icon check.** A wordmark still needs a square. Which two or three letters survive
   the crop? (07's echoed E is already an icon. 02's compressed M is. 10's V is.)

## Then batch 3

Whichever voice lands: three weights, two widths, upper and lower case, and the icon crop —
same seed. Then match it to a real licensed typeface, redraw, and only then decide whether it
needs a symbol at all. Many of these don't.
