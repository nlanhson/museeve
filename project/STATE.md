- **2026-08-11 — Logo batch 1 rejected in full.** All ten marks read "flat, corporate, cold" and "AI-made" to the client. Cause was the register, not the concepts or the palette: every prompt banned gradient, shadow, dimension and texture up front, so all ten came back as flat two-tone geometric marks — the default look every image model produces for the word "logo". **Direction set: type-led with attitude** — the name is the logo, in the lineage of Motown / Blue Note / Def Jam / Warp. Batch 2 → `project/design-system/logo/batch-2-typographic.md`: ten wordmarks, each carrying one concrete formal move (fuse, compress, splice, echo, stretch) rather than a genre costume. Strongest concept is **07, the echo** — the second E drawn as a half-opacity tape-delay repeat of the first, so the doubling in the name becomes the mark and extends into headings, loading states and the app icon. AI lettering is direction-finding only; the winner gets rebuilt from a licensed typeface and redrawn by hand.
